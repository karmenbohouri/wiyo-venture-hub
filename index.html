About us

Wiyo Venture Hub

Our story

In Cameroon’s Littoral Region, youth aged 18 to 35 represent 58% of the labor force, yet an alarming 76% experience invisible underemployment. Driven by high infrastructure costs, severe power outages, and a lack of pre-seed startup funding, thousands of talented young professionals seek to leave the country annually in search of professional dignity.

Wiyoventure Hub was founded to intervene directly at this intersection of economic inclusion and migration mitigation. Instead of viewing youth migration as merely an administrative issue, we build a physical and digital ecosystem where resilient young founders are supported, equipped with modern tools, and connected to diaspora capital to create high-impact businesses right here in Douala.

Our mission

To systematically reduce irregular youth migration in the Littoral region of Cameroon by building an accessible, trust-backed entrepreneurship pipeline. We transform underemployed local youth into resilient, investable business founders by matching them with Cameroonian diaspora mentorship, micro-capital sandboxes and modern digital toolkits.

Our values

Resilience & Dignity

We believe Cameroonian youth possess incredible entrepreneurial drive. We provide the reliable infrastructure (solar power and high-speed internet) required to work with dignity.

Accountability & Progress

Our support and funding are tied strictly to customer discovery and real market validation, instilling operational discipline in early-stage startups.

Community & Diaspora Synergy

We bridge the gap between global Cameroonian diaspora talent and local founders, turning consumer remittance flows into productive business equity.
