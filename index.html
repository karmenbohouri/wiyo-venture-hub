Our mission

To systematically reduce irregular youth migration in the Littoral region of Cameroon by building an accessible, trust-backed entrepreneurship pipeline. We transform underemployed local youth into resilient, investable business founders by matching them with Cameroonian diaspora mentorship, micro-capital sandboxes and modern digital toolkits.

Our story

Creating more pathways for young founders.

Wiyo Venture Hub brings together practical learning, entrepreneurship support, coworking and access to experienced people. Our goal is to help aspiring founders move from ambition to action with a supportive community around them.

Through workshops, one-to-one mentoring and shared workspaces, we want to make it easier for promising ideas to become resilient businesses.
